Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Español English
MimoPet helps you look after your pets (meals, medication, vaccines, parasite treatments, appointments, weight…) and keeps everything on your device. You can use it without an account. If you create an account (free) and get a plan, your pets' data is also copied to our cloud so you don't lose it and so you can share it with the people you invite (your partner, your children, whoever looks after them when you're away…). Without a plan, the app shows ads.

Controller

The data controller is Jonatan Ortega González (devlicius), based in Spain. For any question about your data, write to devlicius.online@gmail.com.

Without an account: everything on your phone

Stays on your phone

If you don't sign in, everything you enter is stored only on your device, in a local database:

  • Your pets: name, species, breed, sex, date of birth, weight, microchip, whether they are neutered, photo and notes.
  • Their care: meals, treatments and the doses given, vaccines, parasite collars, deworming, weight records and vet and grooming appointments, with their notes.
  • Your preferences: language, theme and reminders.

As long as you don't have a plan, none of this is sent to any server; the only way it leaves your phone is if you export a copy and choose to share it. Meals and doses older than one year are deleted automatically.

Account (optional and free)

You can create an account with your email and password, with Google or, on iPhone, with Apple. We store:

  • Your email, an internal account identifier and the sign-in method. With Google or Apple, also the name they provide.
  • The display name shown to your family members (your name or the part of your email before the @) and the app language, so we can write notifications in your language.

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). Having an account does not upload your pets' data: that only happens with a plan.

Cloud and shared family (with a plan)

With an active plan (Individual, Couple or Family), the data listed under "Without an account" is uploaded to our cloud and kept in sync across your family's phones. It is stored in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud), on servers in the European Union (Belgium). Your pets' photos are not uploaded: they stay on the phone where you chose them. Each phone's preferences are not uploaded either.

  • The plan is paid by the family's owner. With the Couple (2 people) and Family (up to 5) plans, the owner can invite other people with a code that expires after 48 hours.
  • Family members can see and change all of the family's data, and each one sees the others' names. Only members can read it.
  • Any member can leave the family (keeping a copy on their phone or not), and the owner can remove someone. If the owner deletes their account, the family is deleted for everyone; each person keeps what is on their phone.
  • If the plan ends, the family becomes read-only. 12 months after the owner's plan ends, the cloud copy is deleted automatically. Nothing on the phones is touched.
  • Meals and doses are deleted from the cloud one year after their date, just as on the phone.

Notifications between members (Couple and Family)

When someone else in your family gives a dose of medication, feeds a pet, changes a treatment, or adds, changes or cancels a vet or grooming appointment in the coming days, our server sends you a notification with their name and the change.

When someone marks a dose as given, your phone removes the reminder for that dose so it isn't given twice. This happens even if you have turned notifications off: in that case the message arrives with no text or sound and only removes the reminder.

To do this we store your devices' notification identifier (Firebase Cloud Messaging), the platform, the language and your preferences (whether you want notifications and for which pets). We also keep a small technical record so we don't send you more than one notification every 10 minutes; it is deleted with the family.

You can turn them off in Settings → Account and family → Your account → Shared family → Family notices or in your phone's notification settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Payments are charged by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card. We use RevenueCat to know which plan you have: it receives your internal account identifier and the store's purchase receipts, but not your email or your pets' data. We store in our database which plan you have and until when.

Ads (only without a plan)

If neither you nor your family has an active plan, the app shows a Google AdMob banner.

  • We request non-personalised ads suitable for all audiences. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before requesting any ad the app shows you Google's consent form, and you can change your choice from the app's settings.
  • Even when non-personalised, Google may use limited technical data (for example, to count impressions and prevent fraud).
  • On iOS, before the first ad the app asks for Apple's tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, AdMob does not use your device's advertising identifier. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • No information about your pets or their care is ever passed to AdMob.
  • With an active plan, yours or your family's, no ads are shown.

Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS). Our database rules prevent anyone other than its members from reading a family's data. The app uses Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android, App Attest/DeviceCheck on iOS), which sends Google proof that the request comes from the genuine app, with no additional personal data.

Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for the account, the cloud, the shared family, notifications between members and subscriptions.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for ads and notifications, which you give through Google's form and your phone's permission, and which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in protecting the service against abuse (App Check and the limit on attempts with invitation codes).

Providers and transfers

We use these providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Tasks, Firebase Cloud Messaging, App Check and AdMob.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription status.

Apple and Google, as stores, process payments as independent controllers. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States). In that case, the transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Data on the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app (meals and doses, one year at most).
  • Account: until you delete it.
  • Cloud copy: while the family owner has a plan and up to 12 months after it ends.
  • Meals and doses in the cloud: one year from their date.
  • Invitation codes: 48 hours at most. Record of redemption attempts: 1 hour.

Your rights

You can access, rectify and delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, request its portability and withdraw your consent at any time. You can exercise most of them from the app itself (Settings → Backup → Export data gives you a full copy). For the rest, write to devlicius.online@gmail.com. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the supervisory authority in your country.

Backups and sharing

You can export all your data to a file (JSON). It is created on your device and you decide where to save it and who to share it with. When you import a copy, its data replaces the app's (and, with a plan, the family's data in the cloud). The button to share the invitation code opens your phone's share menu only when you tap it.

Minors

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 14, who must not create an account. If you believe a minor has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: in Settings → Account and family → Your account → Delete account. Your account and your cloud data are deleted immediately; if you are the owner, the family is deleted for all its members too. The data on your phone is kept.
  • Delete data: you can delete any record, or a pet with everything that belongs to it; with a plan, it is also deleted for the whole family. Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone.

More details on the account deletion page.

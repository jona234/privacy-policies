How to delete your MimoPet account

Last updated: November 2026
Español English
MimoPet is a pet care app developed by Jonatan Ortega González (devlicius). An account is optional. If you have one, you can delete it yourself at any time from the app, without having to write to us.

Delete your account from the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account and family, open Your account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm your identity (with Google, with Apple or by typing your password) and accept.

Deletion is immediate.

Can't access the app?

Write from your account's email to devlicius.online@gmail.com with the subject "Delete MimoPet account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days at most.

What is deleted

Immediate and permanent deletion
  • Your sign-in account (email, name and sign-in method). If you used Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is also revoked.
  • If you are a family's owner: the whole family in the cloud (pets, meals, treatments and doses, vaccines, parasite treatments, appointments, weights…), for all its members, and its pending invitation codes. The other members stop sharing it and keep what is on their phones.
  • If you are a member of someone else's family: you leave it; that family's data still belongs to its owner.
  • Your devices' notification identifiers and your notification preferences.
  • Your subscription status in our database and your customer record at RevenueCat.

What is not affected

  • The data on your phone is kept so you can keep using the app without an account. To delete it too, delete your pets in the app (they are deleted with all their records) or uninstall the app.
  • Your subscription is not cancelled: billing is handled by Apple or Google. Cancel it from your store's subscription settings.
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat may keep their own purchase records according to their policies and legal obligations.

Deleting data without deleting the account

You can delete any record, or a pet with everything that belongs to it, from the app itself; with a plan, it is also deleted from the cloud for the whole family. In addition, the cloud copy is deleted automatically 12 months after the owner's plan ends, and meals and doses one year after their date.

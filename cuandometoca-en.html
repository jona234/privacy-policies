Privacy Policy

CuandoMeToca keeps your medications and doses on your device. You can use it without an account. If you create an account (free) and take out a Premium plan, your data is also copied to our cloud so you don't lose it and, if you wish, so that someone you trust can see whether you took your medication. Without a plan, the app shows ads.

Data controller

The data controller is Jonatan Ortega González (devlicius), based in Spain. For any question about your data, write to devlicius.online@gmail.com.

Without an account: everything on your phone

Never leaves the phone

If you don't sign in, everything you enter is stored only on your device, in a local database:

  • Medications: name, dose, notes, colour, times or interval, units in stock and treatment dates.
  • Your dose history.
  • Appointments and reminders, and weight entries.

This is health data. Unless you have a Premium plan it is not sent to any server; the only way it leaves the phone is if you export a copy and choose to share it.

Account (optional and free)

You can create an account with your email and password, with Google or with Apple. We store:

  • Your email, an internal account ID and the sign-in method. With Google or Apple, also the name they provide.
  • Your time zone (for example, Europe/London), so your schedules are read in your local time.

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). Having an account does not upload your medications: that only happens with a Premium plan.

Cloud copy (Premium plans)

With an active Premium or Premium Caregiver plan, the app uploads your medications, doses, appointments and weight entries to our cloud and keeps them in sync across your devices. They are stored in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud), on servers in the European Union (Belgium), and only you can read them with your account.

  • If the plan ends, the app stops uploading, but you can still download or delete what is in the cloud.
  • 12 months after your last plan ends, the cloud copy is deleted automatically. The app warns you a month before. What is on your phone is not touched.

Caregiver mode (Premium Caregiver plan)

With Premium Caregiver you can create an invitation code (valid for 48 hours) and give it to whoever you want to see your medication. Whoever redeems it, with their own free account, becomes your caregiver:

  • Your caregiver sees your medications and your doses. They do not see your appointments or your weight, and cannot change anything.
  • Each of you sees the name or email of the other's account, so you know who you are linked to.
  • Either of you can end the link at any time. If your plan ends, access stops automatically.
  • Caregiver alerts: if a dose goes unmarked for 30 minutes, our server sends your caregivers a notification with your name, the medication and the time. For this we store the notification ID (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and the language of the caregiver's phone. The record of which alerts were sent is deleted after 2 days.

Subscriptions and payments

Payments are taken by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card. We use RevenueCat to know which plan you have: it receives your internal account ID and the store's purchase receipts, but not your email or your health data. We store which plan you have and until when.

Ads (only without a plan)

If you don't have a Premium plan, the app shows a Google AdMob banner. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before requesting any ad the app shows Google's consent form, where you choose whether to accept personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in ⋮ → Ad privacy.

  • On Android, AdMob may use the device's advertising ID according to what you accepted.
  • On iOS we don't ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so Apple's advertising identifier is not used.
  • Information about your medication or doses is never passed to AdMob.

With an active plan no ads are shown and the ads SDK is not started.

Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS). Our database rules stop anyone from reading your data except you and, in caregiver mode, the people you have linked. The app uses Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android, DeviceCheck on iOS), which sends Google proof that the request comes from the genuine app, with no additional personal data.

Legal basis

  • Your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) to process your health data in the cloud and share it with your caregivers: you give it when you take out the plan and turn these features on, and you can withdraw it by deleting the data or the account.
  • Performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for the account and subscriptions.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised ads.
  • Our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in protecting the service against abuse (App Check).

Providers and transfers

We use these providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, App Check and AdMob.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription status.

Apple and Google, as app stores, process payments as independent controllers. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States). In that case the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Data on the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account: until you delete it.
  • Cloud copy: while you have a plan and up to 12 months after it ends.
  • Invitation codes: 48 hours at most. Record of alerts sent: 2 days.

Your rights

You can access, rectify and erase your data, object to or restrict its processing, request portability and withdraw your consent at any time. You can do most of this in the app itself (⋮ → Export data gives you a full copy). For anything else, write to devlicius.online@gmail.com. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the authority in your country.

Backups and reports

You can export all your data to a JSON file or create a PDF report. They are created on your device and you decide who to share them with. When you import a backup, its data replaces the data in the app (and, with a plan, in the cloud).

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 14, who should not create an account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the account: in ⋮ → Account → Delete account. Your account, your cloud copy and your caregiver links are deleted immediately. The data on the phone is kept.
  • Delete only the data: ⋮ → Delete all data deletes the data on the phone and, if you are signed in, the cloud copy too.

More details on the account deletion page.

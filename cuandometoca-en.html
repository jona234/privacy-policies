CuandoMeToca keeps your medications and your logged doses on your device at all times. There is no account, no server and no advertising: the app does not access the internet, so your data cannot leave your phone unless you choose to export it.

Data we collect

None

The app needs no sign-up and no account. Everything you enter is stored on your device only, in a local database:

  • The name, dose, notes and colour of each medication.
  • Schedules, intervals and units in stock.
  • The doses you log and the treatment dates used for the adherence statistics.

None of it is sent to any server, ours or anyone else’s. We have no way of seeing it.

Permissions the app uses

🔔
NotificationsTo remind you when a dose is due, plus the +30 and +60 minute follow-ups.
⏰
Exact alarmsOn Android 12 and later, so the reminder arrives on time rather than whenever the system decides.
🔋
Battery optimisationOptional. Only requested if you turn it on from the in-app notice, so the system does not delay your reminders.
🔄
Device start-upTo reschedule your reminders after the phone restarts.
📂
File pickerUsed only at the moment you pick a file to import a backup.
🌐
No internet accessOn Android the app does not even declare the internet permission. Fonts ship inside the app, so nothing is downloaded.

Backups

You can export all your data to a JSON file. The file is created on your device and you decide when and with whom to share it (email, messaging, cloud storage…). The app never sends it anywhere on its own.

Importing a file replaces the data currently in the app. The whole operation happens on your device.

Home screen widget (Android)

The widget shows the doses still due today. It reads that list from the phone’s own local storage; it does not contact any server.

Advertising and analytics

CuandoMeToca shows no advertising and includes no analytics, measurement or tracking tooling. It uses neither the device advertising identifier nor any other identifier.

✅ There are no third-party SDKs collecting data. We share nothing with anyone because we collect nothing.

Health data

The medication names, doses and logged intakes you enter are data about your health. That is exactly why the app is built so they never leave the device: they are not synced, not copied to any server and not shared with third parties. The only way they leave your phone is if you create a backup and decide to share it.

Children

The app collects no data from any user, children included.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device only, you can delete any entry inside the app, or uninstall it to remove all data permanently. There is no need to ask us: we hold no copy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The version in force will always be published at this same URL.

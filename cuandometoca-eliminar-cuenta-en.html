How to delete your CuandoMeToca account

Last updated: October 2026
Español English Deutsch 简体中文 Català Euskara Italiano Français
CuandoMeToca is a medication reminder app developed by Jonatan Ortega González (devlicius). The account is optional. If you have one, you can delete it yourself at any time from the app, without writing to us.

Delete your account from the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm it is you (with Google, with Apple or by typing your password) and accept.

The deletion is immediate.

Can't access the app?

Write from your account's email to devlicius.online@gmail.com with the subject "Delete CuandoMeToca account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days at most.

What is deleted

Immediate and permanent
  • Your sign-in account (email, name and sign-in method). If you used Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is also revoked.
  • Your whole cloud copy: medications, doses, appointments and weight.
  • Your caregiver links, both ways, and any pending invitation codes. Whoever looked after you stops seeing your medication.
  • Your devices' notification IDs and your time zone.
  • Your subscription status in our database and your customer record at RevenueCat.

What is not affected

The data on your phone is kept so you can keep using the app without an account. To delete it too, use ⋮ → Delete all data or uninstall the app.

Your subscription is not cancelled: billing is handled by Apple or Google. Cancel it from your store's subscription settings.

Apple, Google and RevenueCat may keep their own records of purchases under their policies and legal obligations.

Delete the data but keep the account

⋮ → Delete all data deletes the data on the phone and, if you are signed in, the cloud copy too, but keeps your account. The cloud copy is also deleted on its own 12 months after your last plan ends.
